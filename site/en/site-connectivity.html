Three things to assess before choosing site connectivity

Space, existing infrastructure and operating needs guide the choice of connectivity.

Space and movement

Above-ground areas, underground spaces, open rooms and winding corridors have different connectivity conditions. Begin with device mobility and where data is generated.

Existing infrastructure

Power lines, network cabling and wireless equipment form the starting point. PLC uses power lines for data communication, but wiring and electrical conditions must be evaluated.

Operations and maintenance

Device count, data requirements and maintenance procedures also matter. Wi-Fi mesh placement and private 5G design should follow these operating needs.

Combine technologies around conditions

ARAD evaluates private 5G, Wi-Fi mesh and PLC around site conditions. Deployment requirements come before treating a technology’s maximum specification as on-site performance.
