Why look at GPUs and networks together?

Data movement between computation steps is part of understanding AI workload performance.

ARAD Networks

In this article

The time between computation

AI workloads include data movement and communication as well as computation. Congestion and queues in shared networks can affect performance, but networking is not the cause of every delay.

Logical paths and transmission queues

Separating routing domains and managing hardware transmission queues are related challenges. ARAD is evaluating how AMLI links logical workload paths to hardware queues.

Compare under controlled conditions

Throughput and latency comparisons require controlled workloads, data sizes, concurrency, switch configurations and measurement intervals. Conditions matter as much as the resulting numbers.

Current evaluation status

AMLI performance effects are under validation. GPU utilisation gains and throughput improvements are not claimed at this stage. Evaluation begins by defining the operating environment and measurement criteria.
