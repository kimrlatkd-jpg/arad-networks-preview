Where does network separation begin?

Start by understanding how devices communicate, not just what devices exist.

ARAD Networks

In this article

From inventory to relationships

A name and an IP address do not describe a device’s communication needs. Its services, ownership and remote access requirements are the starting points for defining boundaries.

Shared infrastructure, different purposes

A camera communicating with a monitoring server has different requirements from an employee workstation. Separation is about defining required communication, rather than removing every connection.

Tunnels and routing spaces

ARAD’s SPN combines per-device tunnels with separate routing spaces. It defines individual paths and permitted destinations through policy. This does not automatically replace encryption or other security controls.

What to establish first

Prepare communication maps, equipment details, change constraints and rollout requirements. Include ongoing management and maintenance in the evaluation.
